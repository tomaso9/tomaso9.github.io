My work is motivated by a practical concern: How can governing arrangements be designed to facilitate responses to complex social-ecological problems? 

I study how governments design rules to facilitate collaboration with other governments and stakeholders. For the most part, my researched has centered on water-related issues. From a governance perspective, managing natural resources that cross political jurisdictions, are used by a variety of actors with different interests and resources, and are subject to environmental pressures requires collaboration and policies that reflect local conditions. I am interested in how those policies are designed, enforced, and how key actors interact to govern those resources.
